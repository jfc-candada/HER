# Public Release Boundary

HER's public release is intentionally narrower than its complete development record.

## Publicly documented

The public project may describe:

- the musical objective and origin of HER
- Phase 1 and Phase 2
- major architectural responsibilities
- demonstrated capabilities and proof-of-concept results
- development methodology
- qualification philosophy
- human-authority and containment principles
- the existence of a durable local development knowledge process
- the direction of future engineering work

These descriptions explain **what HER is, what it has demonstrated, and how the program is structured**.

## Retained privately

The private development environment retains material such as:

- proprietary implementation details
- mathematical formulations and equations
- parameterization and weighting
- differentiated decision logic
- detailed ranking and selection mechanisms
- internal thresholds and tuning
- implementation-specific prompt and control strategies
- detailed operational topology
- private network schematics and host-specific information
- private test evidence and forensic material
- personal or environment-specific information

The public project therefore does not attempt to provide a complete reproducible recipe for every differentiated part of HER.

## The governing distinction

The public documentation should explain **what HER accomplishes** without unnecessarily disclosing **how its proprietary mechanisms accomplish it**.

Capability can be demonstrated at a black-box level.

Implementation details that constitute differentiated intellectual property remain private.

This boundary is part of the engineering discipline of the project, not an afterthought to publication.
